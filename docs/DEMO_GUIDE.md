# TerraCouncil — jury demonstration path (evidence-led)

## Intended scientific demonstration
Open the local council, define the Boké pilot AOI and choose a substantive scenario such as:
1. *What conditions would make a mine extension compatible with community water access, agriculture and forest protection?*
2. *Which evidence would be required to distinguish regional dust episodes from mine-specific PM10 impacts?*
3. *Where do a historic mining footprint, a functional open-pit/dump classification and a cadastral title actually agree or differ?*

### Live flow
- Show exact AOI, period, question, simulated stakeholder perspectives and evidence source selection.
- Open FAITH recovery status, then newly sourced public API observations with retrieval timestamps and QA state.
- Review several independently measured/estimated indicators for true comparability, not fabricated debate tension.
- Explicitly approve the Meta call; Spark states arguments, Wonder challenges unsupported statements, Spark revises.
- Show recorded disagreements, evidence IDs and abstentions; open the human-reviewed French oral rendering only if the session-specific audio was authorized.
- Compare the *same question* before/after newly accepted evidence, including unchanged findings.

## Never claim until performed
- Local scanner tests passing, unless command output actually saved.
- New WEkEO/CAMS/OpenAQ observations for Boké, unless the real provider reply is retrieved and validated.
- Cheng-classified Guinea rasters, until exact spatial results verified.
- Site-specific PM10 measurement without a real station.
- Full production deployment or official representation/endorsement of communities.
- A 10-agent council unless all agents have actually deliberated.

## Public data boundaries
FAITH legacy assets and restrictions remain separate. New hackathon connectors and source selection are the competition contribution; original data is not represented as created during competition.
