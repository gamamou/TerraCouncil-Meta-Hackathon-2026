# FSDC scientific evidence policy

## Evidence gates

Every scientific claim must reference an admissible source with data provider, dataset, version/date, spatial and temporal coverage, units, processing method, licence, hash or identifier, QA and uncertainty.

Distinguish:
- **Observation:** directly supported measurement or verified record.
- **Proxy:** indirect indicator with documented limits.
- **Hypothesis:** interpretation that requires testing.
- **Abstention:** evidence insufficient for a defensible conclusion.

## Boké example limitations

- **NOT_FIELD_VALIDATED** is the scientific state, not a confidence score.
- Missing admissible mining evidence must trigger abstention on mining impacts; it is not proof of absence of mining.
- WorldCover 2021 describes land cover, **not biodiversity condition**.
- WorldPop 2026 estimates population distribution; by itself it is **not a measure of hazard exposure**.
- A single day of CHIRPS precipitation does **not** establish climate risk.
- Geographic and temporal mismatches are disclosed explicitly.

## Visuals and audio

Generated illustrations and avatars must be labeled as AI-generated and never displayed as field observations or satellite evidence. The voice rendering is a dramatized editorial rendering of a documented scientific exchange, not a verbatim recording of a model's internal thoughts.

## Public release gate

Publish only datasets and derivative artifacts with verified redistribution rights; preserve unredacted raw source data and credentials outside the public repository. Use synthetic examples as clearly labeled placeholders when licenses are uncertain.
